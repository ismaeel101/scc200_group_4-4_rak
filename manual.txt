OptiRoute — User Manual
========================
Group 4-4 | SCC200 Group Project

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT IS OPTIROUTE?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OptiRoute is a regional public transport journey planner
for bus and rail services across the North West of England.
Unlike standard journey planners, OptiRoute shows you how
reliable each route is, not just how fast it is.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PLANNING A JOURNEY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. Open http://localhost:5173 in your browser
2. Enter your origin stop or station in the "From" field
3. Enter your destination in the "To" field
4. Select a date and time
5. Choose "Depart at" or "Arrive by"
6. Select a transport mode: All Modes, Bus, Rail, or Mixed
7. Click "Find Routes"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
READING YOUR RESULTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Each journey result shows:
- Departure and arrival time
- Total journey duration
- Number of changes required
- Transport mode(s) used
- Reliability rating: High, Medium, or Low

High  = strong on-time performance record
Medium = some history of delays
Low   = frequent delays or disruptions recorded

Click "Show details" on any result to see the full
leg-by-leg journey breakdown.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
USING THE MAP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
- The map on the right shows your selected route
- All stops and stations are marked
- Your selected journey is highlighted on the map
- Disrupted services are visually marked where data
  is available

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
RELIABILITY SCORES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Reliability scores are calculated using:
- Historical on-time performance data
- Live disruption feeds (where available)
- Regional weather conditions

These are advisory indicators only and are not
guaranteed predictions of service performance.

If live data is unavailable, the app automatically
falls back to scheduled timetable data and displays
a notice to inform you.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WEATHER INFORMATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Current regional weather conditions are shown as a
contextual factor alongside reliability scores.
Adverse weather may reduce the reliability rating
of affected services.

If weather data is temporarily unavailable, journey
planning continues to work normally.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
BUYING TICKETS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
OptiRoute does not sell tickets directly.
Each journey result includes an external link to the
relevant operator's ticketing website.
Click the link to be taken to the operator site
where you can purchase tickets.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
- If your stop name is ambiguous, a dropdown will
  appear — select the correct stop from the list
- For multi-leg journeys, check the reliability score
  of each connection, not just the overall score
- Use "Mixed" transport mode for the most route options
- If results seem slow, the app may be fetching live
  data — wait a moment and results will appear

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
KNOWN LIMITATIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
- Coverage is limited to the North West region only
- Walking is only supported as a transfer between stops,
  not as a standalone journey mode
- Ticket purchasing is handled externally by operators
- Live data depends on third-party feed availability
